# Privacy Policy

**Nomisma: Currency Converter** does not collect, store, or transmit any
personal data.

## What Nomisma does with your data

- **Page content is processed locally.** Nomisma scans the text of pages you
  visit to find prices, and does this entirely inside your browser. The content
  of the pages you visit is never sent anywhere.
- **No accounts, no analytics, no tracking.** There is no sign-in, no usage
  analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind.
- **No personal data is collected.** Nomisma does not read or store your
  browsing history, form input, credentials, or any other personal information.

## Network requests

Nomisma makes exactly one kind of network request: a daily fetch of exchange
rates.

- Endpoint: `https://latest.currency-api.pages.dev` (with a
  `https://cdn.jsdelivr.net` mirror as fallback).
- What is sent: the currency code you selected (for example `USD`). Nothing
  about you or the pages you visit is included.
- The response (a table of exchange rates) is cached locally on your device and
  reused for the rest of the day.

## Data stored on your device

Your settings (target currency, display mode, abbreviation, and any sites you
disabled) are stored using the browser's built-in storage so Nomisma can
remember your preferences. You can clear them at any time by removing the
extension.

## Permissions

- **Storage** is used only to save your settings.
- **Access to all sites** is required because Nomisma works on any website,
  converting prices wherever they appear. The page content is only read and
  annotated locally; it is never uploaded.
